# AI Product Contract Review Questions

Issue-spotting questions for the people responsible for contractual/legal review. This is not legal advice or model contract language.

## Product scope
- Is the service/plan clear?
- Are AI features governed by separate terms?
- Can relevant terms change during subscription?

## Customer information
- What terms govern customer content/data?
- Are training, improvement, evaluation or analytics uses addressed?
- What deletion/return obligations exist?
- Are relevant subprocessors addressed?

## Security
- What security obligations are stated?
- What incident-notification commitments apply?
- Are relevant assurance materials incorporated where needed?

## AI functionality
- What limitations/disclaimers matter to the use?
- Are model/provider changes addressed?
- Are customer responsibilities for output review clear?

## Continuity
- Can functionality be changed/discontinued?
- What availability commitments apply, if any?
- What happens to information at termination?
- What export options exist?

## Risk allocation
- Which warranty disclaimers, indemnities, liability limitations or restrictions require qualified review?
- Do organizational insurance/procurement requirements apply?

Record questions requiring clarification rather than interpreting unfamiliar contractual language without appropriate expertise.
