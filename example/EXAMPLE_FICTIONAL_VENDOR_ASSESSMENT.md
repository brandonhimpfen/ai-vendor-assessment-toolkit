# Example: Fictional AI Vendor Assessment

> Entirely fictional. “Northstar Draft” and “Example Systems Ltd.” are invented for this example.

**Product:** Northstar Draft  
**Vendor:** Example Systems Ltd.  
**Use case:** First drafts of routine internal communications  
**Date:** 2026-08-31

## Proposed use
Five communications employees use the product to draft from non-sensitive briefing notes. A human reviews and edits every output before publication. Confidential personnel matters, legal advice, customer records, credentials and automated publication are excluded.

## Findings

### Information handling
The fictional vendor states prompts/outputs are retained 30 days on the fictional selected plan. The team must confirm fit with organizational requirements.

### AI providers
A fictional third-party language-model provider is used. Downstream retention behavior remains to be confirmed.

### Human oversight
Outputs are drafts only. Users can edit/reject them and there is no publishing integration in the proposed configuration.

### Output quality
Risks include fabricated details, inappropriate tone and omitted context. Proposed control: mandatory review against source briefing information.

### Security
The fictional product supports MFA and role-based accounts. No overall security conclusion is drawn solely from those features.

## Example risk record
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Control | Remaining concern |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Fabricated statement | 3 | 3 | 9 | Mandatory review | Subtle error missed |
| Sensitive data entered | 2 | 4 | 8 | Restriction + training | User behavior |
| Vendor/model change | 2 | 3 | 6 | Periodic review | Notification unclear |

## Example decision
**Pilot only, with conditions:** limit access to identified users; prohibit sensitive uses; require human review; confirm downstream retention before activation; conduct post-implementation review.

This is not a recommendation for any real vendor or organization.
