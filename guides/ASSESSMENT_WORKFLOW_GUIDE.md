# AI Vendor Assessment Workflow Guide

## 1. Start with the use case
Assess what the organization actually wants to do, not merely whether a vendor is well known.

## 2. Complete intake
Record need, users, AI functions, information, integrations and decision impact.

## 3. Screen the use case
Identify sensitive information, consequential decisions, automated actions, extensive integrations or other circumstances needing deeper/specialist review.

## 4. Gather vendor information
Use relevant questionnaires. Ask for evidence where useful. Record unknowns rather than assumptions.

## 5. Review internal fit
Consider your own permissions, policies, information practices, training, oversight and monitoring capability.

## 6. Assess risk
Describe plausible failures and controls. Numeric scoring should not override serious unresolved issues.

## 7. Review contractual questions
Escalate issues to those responsible for contractual/legal review.

## 8. Decide
Record approval, conditions, pilot restrictions, deferral, rejection or escalation. Define the exact scope.

## 9. Configure and implement
Apply approved settings, permissions, retention choices, user instructions and oversight controls.

## 10. Review after implementation
Compare actual use with approved use. Capture errors, complaints, vendor changes and unexpected information flows.

## 11. Reassess material changes
Review when use case, model/provider, capabilities, integrations, data handling, terms or affected population materially changes.
