# Risk Scoring Methodology Guide

The scoring worksheet is a simple prioritization aid, not a validated risk model, compliance test or certification.

## Likelihood
1 Unlikely; 2 Possible; 3 Plausible; 4 Likely; 5 Frequent/strongly expected.

## Impact
1 Limited; 2 Minor; 3 Moderate; 4 Major; 5 Severe.

## Control strength
0 None identified; 1 Weak; 2 Partial; 3 Reasonable with limitations; 4 Strong; 5 Strong and supported by relevant evidence.

## Calculation
**Inherent score = Likelihood × Impact**

Control strength is recorded separately. Do not subtract it mechanically to declare a risk safe. Consider evidence and remaining concerns together.

## Why simple
Universal numerical models can create false precision. Organizations differ in obligations, risk appetite, affected populations and consequences.

## Stop conditions
Regardless of score, unresolved issues may warrant escalation or rejection when necessary authority, information, controls, expertise or confidence is absent.
