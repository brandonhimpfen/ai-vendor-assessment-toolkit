# Customization Guide

1. **Select rather than blindly complete.** Not every questionnaire is needed for every product.
2. **Replace placeholders.** Search bracketed placeholders such as `[VENDOR]`; Markdown checkboxes are not placeholders.
3. **Add your governance.** Define who requests, assesses, approves, configures, monitors, suspends and retires products.
4. **Align terminology.** Use your organization's established procurement, privacy, security, risk and technology-review terms.
5. **Avoid unsupported claims.** Questionnaire completion does not by itself make a vendor or use case compliant, safe, certified or framework-approved.
6. **Protect assessment records.** Vendor materials may be confidential or security-sensitive.
7. **Keep evidence current.** Record source links, dates, plans and assessment dates.
8. **Define reassessment triggers.** Include material product, model, provider, integration, data-use, contractual and use-case changes.
