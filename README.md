# AI Vendor Assessment Toolkit

Version 1.0.0

A vendor-neutral toolkit for structured evaluation of AI-enabled products and services before adoption and during ongoing use.

## Included

- Product intake and AI use-case assessment
- Vendor, privacy/data, security, model/provider, human-oversight, retention, third-party and risk questionnaires
- Procurement decision, approval/rejection, post-implementation and periodic-review records
- Implementation-readiness and contract-review checklists
- AI Vendor Register, Vendor Comparison Matrix and Risk Scoring Worksheet (CSV)
- Assessment workflow, scoring methodology and customization guides
- Completed fictional vendor assessment example
- Customer licence, disclaimer and version history

## Quick start

1. Read `LICENSE.txt` and `DISCLAIMER.md`.
2. Review `guides/ASSESSMENT_WORKFLOW_GUIDE.md`.
3. Customize the templates for your organization.
4. Begin with product intake and use-case assessment.
5. Select only questionnaires relevant to the proposed use.
6. Record evidence and unknowns rather than guessing.
7. Use scores as decision support, not automatic approval.
8. Document the decision, conditions and reassessment triggers.

This toolkit does not establish compliance with any law, regulation, standard, certification or AI governance framework.
